# The UN framework: SDG 5

Source: [UN SDG 5 targets](../sources/un-sdg5-targets.md)

The UN's SDG 5 goal is **"Achieve gender equality and empower all women and
girls."** Two of its nine targets map most directly onto this team's
challenge — *"How might we empower girls to feel confident, supported, and
able to lead in their community?"*:

- **Target 5.1** — end all forms of discrimination against women and girls.
- **Target 5.5** — ensure women's full participation and equal opportunity
  for leadership in decision-making, tracked by two indicators: the share of
  parliamentary/local-government seats held by women, and the share of women
  in managerial positions.

As of the dates on the UN's page, global tracking on the surrounding targets
stood at: women's parliamentary representation at **27.4%** (January 2026),
women spending **2.5 times** more hours than men on unpaid care work, and
women **10% less likely** to own a mobile phone than men (2025 data).
[UN SDG 5 targets](../sources/un-sdg5-targets.md)

None of these global figures are specific to Los Angeles or South Korea, and
they don't measure the same thing as either region's local evidence below —
they set the frame this team's own findings sit inside, not a number to
quote as if it were local. [UN SDG 5 targets](../sources/un-sdg5-targets.md)
