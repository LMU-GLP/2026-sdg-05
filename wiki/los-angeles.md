# Los Angeles

## Who's already researching and working on this

[Alliance for Girls](../sources/la-girls-research.md) is the most active
research organization on girls and gender-expansive youth in LA County. It
uses youth participatory action research (YPAR) — the girls being studied
design and run the research themselves — and has produced an asset map of
programs and services in South and Southeast LA (Service Planning Areas 6
and 7), an active mental-health study, and a 2026 political-impact report.
[Alliance for Girls](../sources/la-girls-research.md)

[Girls Inc. of Los Angeles](../sources/girls-inc-los-angeles.md) is a running
program: girls-only mentoring across 12 LAUSD campuses in South LA, Watts,
and Venice, serving **2,600+ girls annually**, with self-reported outcomes of
**90%** college-graduation confidence and **87%** belief that active
citizenship matters. [Girls Inc. of Los Angeles](../sources/girls-inc-los-angeles.md)

## The evidence gap

**There is no hard, LA-specific statistic on girls' confidence or leadership
in this wiki's sources.** [Alliance for Girls](../sources/la-girls-research.md)
is explicit that it is a landscape and methods source, not a data source.
Girls Inc. of LA cites a national confidence figure (68% → 55%, see
[Girls' confidence data](girls-confidence-data.md)), not one measured in LA.
And the LA County Women and Girls Initiative — the obvious government
source — sunset on June 30, 2024, so nothing from it is newer than mid-2024.
[Alliance for Girls](../sources/la-girls-research.md)

Finding an LA-specific number is flagged in the source itself as this team's
first outstanding research task.
[Alliance for Girls](../sources/la-girls-research.md)
