# Wiki index — SDG 5: Gender Equality (US pay gap)

Challenge: *How might we empower girls to feel confident, supported, and able
to lead in their community?* This team's angle: the US gender pay gap, as a
contribution to gender equality (SDG 5), not the confidence/leadership
framing directly — see [Where this fits SDG 5](sdg5-fit.md) for how the two
connect.
