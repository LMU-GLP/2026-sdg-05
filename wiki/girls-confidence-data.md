# Girls' confidence data (the Girls' Index / ROX)

Source: [ROX Girls' Index](../sources/rox-girls-index-confidence-data.md)

The one dataset in this wiki with real figures on girls' confidence comes
from *The Girls' Index™*, a survey of **more than 17,500 girls in 5th through
12th grades** in the U.S., run by Ruling Our eXperiences (ROX).
[ROX Girls' Index](../sources/rox-girls-index-confidence-data.md)

## What it found

- Girls who describe themselves as confident fell from **68% (2017) to 55%
  (2023)** nationally.
- **5th grade** saw the steepest drop: **86% (2017) → 68% (2023)**.
- **9th grade** hit the lowest point of any grade: **50% (2023)**, down from
  60% in 2017.
- **12th grade** stayed flat at **62%**.
- **46%** of surveyed girls reported 6+ hours of social media use per day.
- Depression at least four days a week was reported by **~40%** of girls in
  grades 8–12 and **~35%** of girls in grades 5–7.

[ROX Girls' Index](../sources/rox-girls-index-confidence-data.md)

## Where this data does and doesn't reach

This is a **national U.S. sample, not a Los Angeles– or South Korea–specific
one.** No figure in this wiki measures girls' self-reported confidence in LA
or in Korea specifically. The only regional link is that
[Girls Inc. of Los Angeles](existing-programs.md) cites this same national
68%-to-55% decline as the motivation for its LA programming
([source](../sources/girls-inc-los-angeles.md)) — which means an LA program
is built on a national number, not a local one.

Korea's evidence in this wiki ([South Korea](south-korea.md)) is about the
gender pay gap among working-age women, which is a different measurement
entirely — pay, not self-reported confidence — so it cannot be compared to
this dataset's figures.
