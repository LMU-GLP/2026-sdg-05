# Who's already working on this

Two organizations in this wiki are already doing work that touches this
team's challenge directly — one a research method, one a running program.

## Alliance for Girls (Los Angeles / Bay Area) — research method

Alliance for Girls runs **youth participatory action research (YPAR)**: the
girls and gender-expansive youth being studied design the research,
collect the data, and interpret it themselves, and are paid for their
participation.
[Alliance for Girls](../sources/la-girls-research.md)

Its LA County work includes an asset map of programs and services in South
and Southeast LA (Service Planning Areas 6 and 7), an active mental-health
YPAR study, and a 2026 political-impact report. It has **no hard confidence
or leadership statistic of its own** — it's a landscape and methods source,
useful for knowing what already exists in LA, not for a figure to cite.
[Alliance for Girls](../sources/la-girls-research.md)

> "We believe that girls and gender-expansive youth of color deserve the
> space to tell their own stories and inform solutions to challenges that
> affect their lives." [Alliance for Girls](../sources/la-girls-research.md)

Also worth noting: the LA County Women and Girls Initiative — the obvious
government data source — **sunset on June 30, 2024**, so nothing from that
source is newer than mid-2024.
[Alliance for Girls](../sources/la-girls-research.md)

## Girls Inc. of Los Angeles — running program

Girls Inc. of Los Angeles delivers girls-only mentoring and research-based
curricula across **12 LAUSD campuses** in South LA, Watts, and Venice,
serving **2,600+ girls annually**.
[Girls Inc. of Los Angeles](../sources/girls-inc-los-angeles.md)

Its self-reported program outcomes: **91%** of girls know how to support a
friend in emotional distress, **90%** are confident they'll graduate
college, **87%** believe active citizenship is important.
[Girls Inc. of Los Angeles](../sources/girls-inc-los-angeles.md)

Girls Inc. cites the national **68%-to-55%** confidence decline (see
[Girls' confidence data](girls-confidence-data.md)) as why this work matters
— its own outcome numbers are program-reported, not independently verified,
and describe its own participants, not LA girls broadly.
[Girls Inc. of Los Angeles](../sources/girls-inc-los-angeles.md)

> "At Girls Inc., we believe that our girls are inherently strong and
> talented and that it is our role to help them tap into that strength."
> — Erin Ross, CEO
> [Girls Inc. of Los Angeles](../sources/girls-inc-los-angeles.md)

## What this means for a new solution

Both organizations are LA-specific. Nothing in this wiki's sources describes
a comparable running program or research effort in South Korea — that is a
gap, not a finding of "nothing exists there," since no one on this team has
looked yet.
