# UN SDG 5 — Target 5.a (economic resources) and Target 5.1 (discrimination)

**URL:** https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal5
**Date read:** 2026-08-01

## Key figures / targets

**Target 5.a:** "Undertake reforms to give women equal rights to economic
resources, as well as access to ownership and control over land and other
forms of property, financial services, inheritance and natural resources,
in accordance with national laws."
- Indicator 5.a.1: agricultural land ownership/rights by sex.
- Indicator 5.a.2: legal frameworks guaranteeing women's equal land rights.
- 2026 progress: fewer than half of women in agricultural households hold
  secure land rights in nearly 90% of reporting countries; only about 30% of
  countries ensure joint land registration or women's participation in land
  governance; over 40% of countries lack guarantees for spousal consent and
  equal inheritance rights.

**Target 5.1:** "End all forms of discrimination against all women and girls
everywhere."
- Indicator 5.1.1: legal frameworks promoting equality and non-discrimination
  based on sex.
- 2026 status: women globally hold only **64%** of the legal rights enjoyed
  by men, with no country achieving perfect scores across all measured areas.

## Summary

Target 5.a is the UN's official target closest to economic equality, but it
is framed around land, property, and financial-services rights, not wages or
salary specifically — none of its indicators measure a pay gap directly, so
this team's US pay-gap research sits adjacent to this target rather than
inside it.
