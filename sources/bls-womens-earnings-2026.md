# BLS: Usual Weekly Earnings Summary, Q2 2026 (+ full-time employment table)

**URL:** https://www.bls.gov/news.release/wkyeng.nr0.htm (earnings), https://www.bls.gov/web/empsit/cpseea06.htm (headcount)
**Date read:** 2026-08-01
**Published:** 2026-07-21 (earnings release); June 2026 data (headcount table)

## Key figures

- **Headcount:** In June 2026, **59,360 thousand** (59.36 million) women 16
  years and over were employed full-time, vs. **74,255 thousand** (74.255
  million) men.
- **Median weekly earnings, Q2 2026:** Women **$1,131**; men **$1,380**.
  Women's earnings were **82.0%** of men's ("Women had median weekly earnings
  of $1,131, or 82.0 percent of the $1,380 median" for men).
- **By race/ethnicity — but compared WITHIN each race, not to white men:**
  "White women earned 81.6 percent as much as their male counterparts,
  compared with 91.0 percent for Black women, 78.3 percent for Asian women,
  and 83.5 percent for Hispanic women." Each percentage is that race's women
  vs. that same race's men — e.g., Black women vs. Black men, not Black women
  vs. white men.
  - Separately, the release states Black women's median earnings were $991,
    or 86.8% of white women's earnings — a cross-race comparison, but between
    women only, not against any men's figure.

## Summary

BLS's official Q2 2026 earnings ratio (82.0%) lines up closely with
Payscale's uncontrolled figure (82 cents), and BLS gives this wiki its only
actual headcount: 59.36 million women employed full-time. Its race/ethnicity
breakdown looks far more favorable than every other race breakdown in this
wiki (e.g., Black women at 91.0%) only because BLS compares each race's
women to men of that same race — not to white men, which is the baseline
every other source in this wiki uses — so it cannot be read as contradicting
those other figures.
