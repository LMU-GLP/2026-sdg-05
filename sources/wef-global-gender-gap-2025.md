# Global Gender Gap Report 2025

- **Source:** World Economic Forum
- **URL:** https://www.weforum.org/press/2025/06/gender-gap-closes-at-fastest-rate-since-pandemic-but-full-parity-still-over-a-century-away/
- **Secondary URL (South Korea detail):** https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025061310020829668
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- The report covers **148 economies**.
- The **global gender gap score is 68.8%** closed.
- At the current rate, full parity is **123 years away**.
- **Iceland ranks #1**, with **92.6%** of its gender gap closed.
- **Political empowerment** is the widest gap of the four subindexes: only
  **22.9%** closed globally, projected to take **162 years** to close fully.
- **Economic participation and opportunity**: the leading region (Northern
  America) has closed **76.1%** of this gap; globally the dimension has
  gained **5.6 percentage points since 2006**, with full economic parity
  projected to take **135 years**.
- Globally, **women hold only 28.8% of top leadership positions** despite
  representing **41.2% of the global workforce**.

### South Korea (published June 13, 2025)

- Ranked **101st**, down from **94th** the prior year — a fall of **7 places**.
- Economic subindex: ranked **114th**, down from 112th.
- Political subindex: ranked **92nd**, down from 72nd — a fall of 20 places.
- The article states South Korea's overall score is below the global average
  of 0.688, without giving South Korea's exact overall score.

## What this source does NOT give you

No comparable exact overall score for South Korea (only "below 0.688") and
no U.S.-specific figures from the 2025 edition were found in this pass — the
U.S. rank commonly cited online (43rd, 2024 edition) is from the **previous**
year's report, not 2025, so it is not included here as a 2025 figure. Per
the wiki rules, don't quote it as current until a 2025-specific figure is
found and sourced directly.

## Summary

The WEF's 2025 index — a single method applied to 148 economies, which is
what makes country comparisons within it valid — shows global gender parity
still 123 years away, with political empowerment the least-closed dimension
by far (22.9%, 162 years to close). South Korea fell seven places to 101st,
driven mostly by a 20-place drop in its political-empowerment ranking.
