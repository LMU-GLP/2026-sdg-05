# America's Women and the Wage Gap (National Partnership for Women & Families)

**URL:** https://nationalpartnership.org/report/americas-women-and-the-wage-gap/
**Date read:** 2026-08-01

## Key figures

Published March 2026.

- Across all US workers (2024 data), women earn **76 cents** for every dollar paid to a man — a yearly difference of **$14,640**.
- Combined, women in the US lose **almost $1.9 trillion every year** to the wage gap.
- By race/ethnicity, per dollar paid to white, non-Hispanic men:
  - White, non-Hispanic women: **73 cents**
  - Black women: **63 cents**
  - Latina women: **54 cents**
  - Native American women: **53 cents**
  - Asian American/Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander women: **50–83 cents**, varying widely by specific ethnicity (83 cents for the group overall)
  - Bangladeshi women (lowest cited subgroup): **50 cents**

## Quote

> "Every state has a gender wage gap, and it persists regardless of industry
> and education level and within occupations."

## Summary

This report's overall figure (76 cents) is lower than AAUW's and Payscale's
because it counts all workers, not just full-time year-round ones, and it
shows the gap is far worse for Black, Latina, and Native American women than
for white women — the gap is not one number, it's a range that depends heavily
on race.
