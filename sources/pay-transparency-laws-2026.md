# US Pay Transparency Laws by State (CompPort, 2026 guide)

**URL:** https://www.compport.com/blog/usa-pay-transparency-laws-by-state
**Date read:** 2026-08-01

## Key figures

Published 2026-01-13.

- At least **14 states** currently have active pay transparency/wage transparency laws as of 2026: California, Colorado, Connecticut, Hawaii, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Minnesota, Nevada, New Jersey, New York, Rhode Island, Vermont, and Washington.
- These laws typically require employers to "disclose salary ranges in job ads, promotions, and hiring" and provide "a general description of benefits and other compensation offered."
- Employee-count thresholds for coverage range from 4 to 50+ employees depending on the state, and most laws cover remote positions where the work could be performed in that state.

## Quote

> "Pay transparency has moved from a progressive ideal to a legal expectation
> across many U.S. states."

## Summary

Fourteen states plus growing enforcement (not a federal law) currently
require employers to post salary ranges, a state-by-state patchwork solution
that exists precisely because the federal Paycheck Fairness Act has not
passed — most of the country still has no such requirement.
