# Alliance for Girls — Youth Research Hub (Los Angeles work)

- **Source:** Alliance for Girls, a membership alliance of organizations serving girls and gender-expansive youth in the California Bay Area and Los Angeles County
- **URL:** https://www.alliance4girls.org/research
- **Read on:** 2026-07-30

## What this organization is

Alliance for Girls runs **youth participatory action research (YPAR)**: the
girls and gender-expansive youth being studied design the research, collect the
data, and interpret it. Everyone who participates is paid. Their research
directly sets their advocacy agenda.

## Their Los Angeles work

- **"AFG in LA County: The First Years"** (October 2023) — what they learned from six years of gathering data on the needs of girls and gender-expansive youth in LA County, starting 2017. https://www.alliance4girls.org/research/la-first-years
- **"Hear Their Voices: The Experiences of Girls & Gender-Expansive Youth in South and Southeast Los Angeles"** — includes a community asset map with a heat map of programs, services, and resources available in Service Planning Areas (SPAs) 6 and 7.
- **"Girl You Can Do Anything (Los Angeles)"** (November 2020)
- **Current mental health study** — an active YPAR project on how youth experience mental health and safety in their communities. https://www.alliance4girls.org/mental-health-study
- **"From Anticipation to Reality: Political Impact Report 2026"** (May 27, 2026) — tracks how organizations serving girls are contending with heightened threats; documents "a crisis that arrived faster and hit harder than organizations planned for."

## Direct quote

## ⚠️ What this source does NOT give you

**No hard statistic.** This is a landscape and methods source, not a data
source. It tells you who is already working on this in LA and how they gather
evidence — which matters, because it stops the team from building something that
already exists. **Finding an LA-specific number is this team's first job in
Friday's source sprint.** Start with "AFG in LA County: The First Years" and the
asset map, both linked above.

Also note: the LA County Women and Girls Initiative, which would be the obvious
government source, **sunset on June 30, 2024** after eight years. Its reports
are still online but nothing is newer than mid-2024.

## Summary

Alliance for Girls is the most active research organization on girls and
gender-expansive youth in LA County, and its asset map of South and Southeast LA
shows which programs already exist and where the gaps are. Its method is the
point for this team: the youth being served designed the research themselves,
which is a defensible model for how to find out what girls in a community
actually need instead of guessing.
