# Girls Inc. of Los Angeles — program working on this problem now

- **Source:** Girls Inc. of Los Angeles, "What We Do"
- **URL:** https://girlsincla.org/what-we-do
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## What this organization does

Girls Inc. delivers the "Girls Inc. Experience": girls-only safe spaces,
mentoring relationships with trained staff and volunteers, research-based
curricula, and sustained programming from elementary through high school.
Its mission is to inspire "all girls to be strong, smart, and bold," with a
stated vision of **"powerful girls in an equitable society."**

## Key figures

- Serves **2,600+ girls annually** across **12 LAUSD campuses** in South Los
  Angeles, Watts, and Venice.
- **91%** of girls know how to support a friend experiencing emotional
  distress.
- **90%** are confident they will graduate college.
- **87%** believe active citizenship is important.
- Cites, as context for its own programming, that **girls' confidence
  declined from 68% to 55%** over the past decade (this is the same national
  figure reported in the ROX Girls' Index study — see
  [ROX Girls' Index](rox-girls-index-confidence-data.md) — not an
  LA-specific number).
- Also cites, as context: **"1 in 3 young people grows up without a
  mentor"**, and that **women represent only 35% of the STEM workforce**
  despite earning half of science/engineering degrees.
- Fall 2025: piloting a new high school program to expand services to girls
  ages 14–18.

## Summary

Girls Inc. of Los Angeles is an active, already-operating program in this
team's LA candidate region, serving 2,600+ girls a year across 12 LAUSD
campuses with confidence- and leadership-building programming. Its own
90–91% outcome figures are self-reported program statistics, not independent
research, and the confidence decline it cites as motivation is a national
number, not one measured in LA specifically.
