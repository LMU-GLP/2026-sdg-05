# Girls Inc. of Los Angeles

- **Source:** Girls Inc. of Los Angeles (organization website)
- **URL:** https://girlsincla.org/
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## What this organization does

Girls Inc. LA describes itself as "the longest-running girls' leadership
program" in Los Angeles. It runs direct-service programs plus advocacy work,
aimed at helping girls "defy limitations and stereotypes" and become "leaders
of today and tomorrow." It is headquartered at 4821 Lankershim Blvd., Suite F
#110, North Hollywood, CA 91601.

## Key figures

- **91%** of elementary participants reported feeling safe and supported in
  the girls-only environment.
- **86%** of middle school participants agreed there are trustworthy adults
  at Girls Inc.
- **86%** of participants said the organization teaches them content not
  covered in school.

## What this source does NOT give you

No total count of girls served, no budget/funding figures, and no founding
date were on this page. This is a program's own self-reported outcome
percentages, not third-party evaluation data — worth flagging if the team
uses these numbers in a pitch.

## Summary

Girls Inc. of Los Angeles is an existing, currently-operating girls'
leadership program in LA whose own reported numbers (91% feeling safe and
supported, 86% naming a trustworthy adult) speak directly to this team's
challenge of confidence and support. It's evidence that a program targeting
this exact problem in this exact region already exists and reports results.
