# Pregnant Workers Fairness Act (PWFA) — A Better Balance

**URL:** https://www.abetterbalance.org/pregnant-postpartum-workers-know-your-rights/
**Date read:** 2026-08-01
**Effective:** 2023-06-27

## Key facts / quotes

- "Most workers have a right to reasonable accommodations, or temporary
  changes at work, that are medically-necessary during or after pregnancy."
- Covers workers who are pregnant, recovering from childbirth, pumping
  breast milk, or have pregnancy-related medical conditions.
- Guaranteed accommodations include: flexible scheduling for
  prenatal/postnatal appointments, additional/longer breaks, light duty,
  temporary transfer, leave for childbirth recovery (even without FMLA
  qualification), leave for bedrest/miscarriage recovery/postpartum
  depression/mastitis, and remote/telework options.
- Employers "cannot punish or fire you" for requesting these accommodations.
- References "final 2024 regulations" implementing the law.

## Summary

The PWFA (effective 2023) is the broadest federal workplace-flexibility law
in this wiki, guaranteeing schedule changes and even remote work as
accommodations — but only during and shortly after pregnancy, not for
ongoing caregiving responsibilities once a child is past infancy, which is
where the broader workplace-inflexibility cause still has no federal fix.
