# The Girls' Index: girls' self-confidence is dropping, in charts

- **Source:** Ruling Our eXperiences (ROX), *The Girls' Index™* survey, as
  reported by Sarah D. Sparks, *Education Week*, "The State of Girls' Mental
  Health and Self-Confidence, in Charts," November 2023
- **URL:** https://www.edweek.org/leadership/the-state-of-girls-mental-health-and-self-confidence-in-charts/2023/11
- **Related URL (original research org):** https://www.rulingourexperiences.com/research
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- The survey covers **"more than 17,500 girls in 5th through 12th grades"**
  across the U.S. — a national, not a Los Angeles– or Korea-specific, sample.
- Girls who describe themselves as confident dropped from **68% (2017) to
  55% (2023)** — a national-average decline over six years.
- **5th grade:** confidence fell from **86% (2017) to 68% (2023)** — the
  largest drop of any grade band.
- **9th grade:** confidence hit its lowest point, **50% (2023), down from 60%
  (2017)**.
- **12th grade:** confidence was **62%, unchanged** from 2017 to 2023.
- **"46 percent reported spending six or more hours per day on social media
  platforms."**
- **"About 40 percent of girls in 8th through 12th grades and about 35
  percent in 5th through 7th grades reported feeling depressed at least four
  days per week."**
- The original research org, ROX, describes the full Girls' Index sample as
  drawing on **"insights from 17,000 girls"** via a **"nationally-representative
  sample"**, and separately states **"39% don't feel like they belong"** and
  **"57% don't believe they're smart enough."**

## Summary

This is a national U.S. dataset, not one specific to Los Angeles or South
Korea, but it is the real-figures backbone behind the confidence language in
this team's challenge: self-reported girls' confidence fell six years running,
with the steepest drop happening right at the transition into middle school
(5th grade, 86% → 68%) and the low point in early high school (9th grade,
50%). Girls Inc. of Los Angeles cites this same 68%-to-55% national decline
as the problem its LA programming is built to counter (see
[Girls Inc. of Los Angeles](girls-inc-los-angeles.md)), which is the only
link connecting this dataset to either of this team's candidate regions.
