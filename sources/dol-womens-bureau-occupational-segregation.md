# DOL Women's Bureau: Equal Pay Act & Title IX anniversary roundtable

**URL:** https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/wb/wb20230613
**Date read:** 2026-08-01
**Published:** 2023-06-13

## Key facts / quotes

- Women's Bureau Director Wendy Chun-Hoon: "Too often, women and people of
  color are shut out of high-paying opportunities... This kind of
  occupational segregation is one of the biggest barriers to economic
  equity for women."
- Same source: "the single largest cause of wage gaps are differences in the
  jobs people hold."
- The release marks the anniversaries of the Equal Pay Act (1963) and Title
  IX (1972), examined via a roundtable on women in professional sports
  careers specifically — it does not provide broader STEM/nontraditional
  occupation statistics or program figures.

## Summary

The US Department of Labor's own Women's Bureau names occupational
segregation — not pay-setting discrimination — as the single largest driver
of the wage gap, directly matching the cause named in
[the AAUW causes report](aauw-causes-report-2025.md); this source, however,
is a press release marking a legal anniversary, not a program with reach or
outcome figures.
