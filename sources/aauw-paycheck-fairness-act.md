# AAUW: The Paycheck Fairness Act (policy solution)

**URL:** https://www.aauw.org/resources/news/media/press-releases/aauw-applauds-paycheck-fairness-act-introduction-in-congress/
**Date read:** 2026-08-01

## Key figures / facts

- The Paycheck Fairness Act would close loopholes in the **Equal Pay Act of 1963**: it would prohibit employers from using salary history to set pay, and protect workers from retaliation for discussing pay with colleagues.
- It would also require wage-data collection and support salary-negotiation training programs.
- Most recent press release found is dated **2019-01-31** (introduced in both House and Senate that day, sponsored by Rep. Rosa DeLauro and Sen. Patty Murray); a separate AAUW statement dated **2021-06-08** confirms the bill failed a procedural vote in the Senate that year.
- As of a January 2026 industry summary (see [Pay transparency laws by state](pay-transparency-laws-2026.md)), the Act "hasn't passed yet" at the federal level.

## Quote

> "The Paycheck Fairness Act is essential to eradicating practices that have
> perpetuated the pay gap for far too long." — Kim Churches, AAUW CEO (2019-01-31 press release)

## Summary

The Paycheck Fairness Act is a federal bill, repeatedly introduced since at
least 2019 and blocked in the Senate as recently as 2021, that would close
specific loopholes in 1963 equal-pay law — as of 2026 it still has not
become law, which is why the wage gap remains legal to perpetuate through
salary-history questions in most states.
