# DOL: PUMP Act (FLSA protections to pump at work)

**URL:** https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/pump-at-work
**Date read:** 2026-08-01
**Effective:** 2022-12-29 (expanded transportation-worker coverage effective 2025-12-29)

## Key facts / quotes

- "reasonable break time for an employee to express breast milk for her
  nursing child for one year after the child's birth."
- Space must be "shielded from view and free from intrusion from coworkers
  and the public" and "not a bathroom."
- Expanded coverage to previously excluded workers: agricultural workers,
  nurses, teachers, truck and taxi drivers, and home care workers.
- Transportation-industry employers may be exempt "if an employer
  demonstrates that compliance would require significant expense or results
  in unsafe conditions."
- The page does not state whether break time must be paid.

## Summary

The PUMP Act is a narrow, already-passed federal law (2022) addressing one
specific piece of workplace inflexibility for new mothers — break time and
private space to pump — but it doesn't touch scheduling flexibility,
childcare access, or paid leave more broadly, which are the other pieces
named under [workplace inflexibility](aauw-causes-report-2025.md).
