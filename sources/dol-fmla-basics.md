# DOL: Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) basics

**URL:** https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fmla
**Date read:** 2026-08-01

## Key facts / quotes

- Guarantees "up to 12 workweeks of leave in a 12-month period" for standard
  qualifying reasons (serious health conditions, childbirth/adoption/foster
  placement, military family exigencies); military caregiver leave allows
  "up to 26 workweeks."
- The law "entitles eligible employees of covered employers to take
  **unpaid**, job-protected leave" and requires continued group health
  benefits.
- **Covered employers:** private employers with 50+ employees in 20+
  workweeks; all public agencies and local educational agencies regardless
  of size.
- **Eligibility (all three required):** worked for the employer 12+ months;
  1,250+ hours of service in the prior 12 months; works at a location with
  50+ employees within 75 miles.

## Summary

FMLA guarantees job-protected leave but explicitly does not guarantee pay,
and its 50-employee/1,250-hour eligibility rules exclude a large share of
workers by design — see
[FMLA coverage gaps](fmla-1993-coverage-gaps.md) for how many.
