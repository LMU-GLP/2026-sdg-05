# Goal 5: Achieve gender equality and empower all women and girls

- **Source:** United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs — Sustainable Development Goals
- **URL:** https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal5
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## The official targets

- **5.1:** End all forms of discrimination against all women and girls everywhere
- **5.2:** Eliminate all forms of violence against all women and girls in the public and private spheres
- **5.3:** Eliminate all harmful practices, such as child, early and forced marriage and female genital mutilation
- **5.4:** Recognize and value unpaid care and domestic work through public services and social protection policies
- **5.5:** Ensure women's full and effective participation and equal opportunities for leadership at all levels
- **5.6:** Ensure universal access to sexual and reproductive health and reproductive rights
- **5.a:** Undertake reforms to give women equal rights to economic resources and property ownership
- **5.b:** Enhance use of enabling technology to promote women's empowerment
- **5.c:** Adopt and strengthen sound policies for gender equality promotion

Target 5.5 is the one that speaks most directly to this team's challenge: it
names "leadership at all levels" as the explicit goal, not just protection
from harm.

## Key figures (from the page's own facts and figures)

- "Nearly 1 in 3 women – estimated 840 million globally – have experienced
  physical and/or sexual violence"
- "women's share in lower and single chambers of national parliaments reached
  27.4%, up from 22.3% in 2015"
- "In 2025, only 56.3% of women aged 15-49 who are married or in a union have
  full decision-making power"
- "Globally, 78% of women and 87% of men owned a mobile phone in 2025"
- "nearly one in five girls (18%) marry before the age of 18"
- "230 million girls and women alive today have undergone" female genital
  mutilation
- "women and girls were spending an average of 2.5 times more hours per day
  on domestic and care work than men"

## Summary

SDG 5 sets nine targets for gender equality, and Target 5.5 — women's full
participation and equal opportunity for leadership — is the one this team's
challenge sits under. The UN's own figures show the world is still far from
any of these targets: a 27.4% share of parliamentary seats and a 2.5x gap in
unpaid care work are the two figures closest to what "leadership" and
"support" mean in practice.
