# US Dept. of Labor press release (2021-04-26): funding opportunity to place more women in Registered Apprenticeships

**URL:** https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/wb/wb20210426
**Date read:** 2026-08-01
**Published:** 2021-04-26

## Key figures

- "While women make up nearly half of the U.S. labor force, they comprise
  only 12 percent of Registered Apprentices."
- In response, DOL "announced a funding opportunity of $3.5 million for up to
  10 grants to increase and retain the number of women in quality Registered
  Apprenticeship Programs and nontraditional occupations."

## Summary

As of this 2021 announcement — nearly 30 years after the WANTO Act (1992)
first funded exactly this goal — women still held only 12% of Registered
Apprenticeships nationally, against roughly half the labor force. This is the
clearest single data point in this wiki that a federal program aimed
specifically at occupational segregation has not come close to closing that
gap.
