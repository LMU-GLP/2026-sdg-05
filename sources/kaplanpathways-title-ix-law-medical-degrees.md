# Kaplan Pathways: 4 ways Title IX expanded women's access to US higher education

**URL:** https://www.kaplanpathways.com/blog/4-ways-title-ix-expanded-women-access-to-us-higher-education/
**Date read:** 2026-08-01

## Key figures

- In 1972 (the year Title IX passed), "women earned only 7% of all law
  degrees and 9% of all medical degrees; they now earn nearly half of all
  degrees in both areas." (The article does not give an exact year for the
  "now" figure.)

## Summary

Law and medicine are the clearest example of a field where Title IX-driven
access to graduate/professional education visibly narrowed occupational
segregation — from roughly 1 in 13 degree-holders being women in 1972 to
close to half. This contrasts with the AAUW source above, where the
highest-paying *undergraduate* majors (STEM, computer science, engineering)
remain male-dominated as of a 2017 study — meaning Title IX's effect on
occupational segregation varies sharply by field, not a uniform result.
