# US Dept. of Labor, Women's Bureau: Apprenticeships and Nontraditional Occupations (WANTO)

**URL:** https://www.dol.gov/agencies/wb/topics/apprenticeships
**Date read:** 2026-08-01

## Key facts

- The Women in Apprenticeship and Nontraditional Occupations (WANTO) grant
  program, established by the Women in Apprenticeship and Nontraditional
  Occupations Act of 1992, "helps to expand pathways for women to enter and
  lead in all industries by recruiting, mentoring, training, and retaining
  more women in quality pre-apprenticeship and apprenticeship programs."
- Funding: "Since 2017, the Women's Bureau has awarded nearly $31 million to
  50 community-based organizations throughout the country." In 2025, "$5
  million to seven community-based organizations."
- On wages: nontraditional occupations (the trades WANTO targets) "tend to be
  higher-paid than occupations in which women comprise the majority of
  workers" — no specific comparative wage figure given on this page.

## Summary

WANTO is the one federal program in this wiki specifically aimed at moving
women into higher-paying, male-dominated fields (construction, manufacturing,
transportation trades) rather than banning discrimination or funding leave.
It funds outreach/retention grants rather than mandating anything of
employers. See
[DOL press release 2021](dol-wb-2021-women-apprenticeships-12-percent.md) for
the measured outcome — women's underrepresentation in these fields remains
severe despite the program running since 1992.
