# AAUW Work Smart & Start Smart (salary negotiation program)

**URL:** https://www.aauw.org/resources/programs/salary-negotiation/
**Date read:** 2026-08-01

## Key figures

- "AAUW's salary negotiation programs have trained over **190K women** to negotiate their salaries and benefits to date."
- **70%** of Work Smart participants report the workshop had a positive impact on their ability to negotiate a fair salary.
- The program cites women earning "approximately **83 cents** for every dollar a full-time male worker is paid."
- Without intervention, "the pay gap will not close til **2106**."

## Quote

> "70% of Work Smart participants report the workshop had a positive impact
> on their ability to negotiate for a fair salary."

## Summary

AAUW's Work Smart/Start Smart is an existing, running program that teaches
women salary-negotiation skills and has reached over 190,000 women, with most
reporting it helped — a concrete example of an individual-skills intervention,
as distinct from the policy-level solutions like the Paycheck Fairness Act.
