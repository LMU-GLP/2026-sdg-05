# San Francisco Family Friendly Workplace Ordinance (D.Law explainer)

**URL:** https://d.law/blog/what-is-san-franciscos-family-friendly-workplace-ordinance/
**Date read:** 2026-08-01

## Key facts / quotes

- First adopted in 2014; the current, updated version "goes into effect in
  June 2022."
- Covers "employers within San Francisco with more than 20 workers."
- Applies to "any employee of a San Francisco-based employer even if that
  employee does not work in San Francisco."
- Guarantees employees the right to request an "alternative work schedule,
  that allows them to remain fully employed while still being able to care
  for a loved one" — a right to request and a response process, not a
  guaranteed outcome.
- A separate industry summary (not independently verified against a primary
  legal text here) describes the process as: written request, employer must
  respond within 21 days, up to two requests per 12-month period.

## Summary

This is the only *local* (city-level) workplace-flexibility law in this
wiki, and it's the broadest in scope of who it protects (any caregiver, not
just new parents) — but it only reaches employees of San Francisco-based
employers with 20+ workers, a tiny fraction of the US workforce compared to
national laws like the PUMP Act or PWFA.
