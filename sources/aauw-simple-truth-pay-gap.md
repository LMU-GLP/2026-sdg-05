# The Simple Truth About the Gender Pay Gap (AAUW)

**URL:** https://www.aauw.org/resources/research/simple-truth/
**Date read:** 2026-08-01

## Key figures

- Women working full-time earn **81 cents per dollar** earned by men.
- Women working part-time earn **76 cents per dollar** earned by men.
- Over a 40-year career, this costs women **$542,800**.
- Women receive only **81% of average monthly Social Security benefits** compared to men.

## Quote

> "Despite pay discrimination becoming illegal over half a century ago, the pay
> gap between men and women persists—an inequity that continues to hurt our
> nation's workers and our national economy."

## Summary

AAUW's long-running "Simple Truth" report puts the US full-time gender pay
gap at 81 cents on the dollar, with a much wider gap (76 cents) for part-time
workers, and quantifies the lifetime cost at over half a million dollars.
It frames the gap as a persistent inequity despite decades-old anti-discrimination law.
