# Institute for Family Studies: Is Parental Leave to Blame for the Gender Wage Gap?

**URL:** https://ifstudies.org/blog/is-parental-leave-to-blame-for-the-gender-wage-gap
**Date read:** 2026-08-01

## Key figures

- Rate of gender wage convergence before the FMLA (1993): "about a percentage
  point a year."
- Rate after: "about a third of a point per year."
- The author's own independent replication: "both groups saw their gender gap
  drop by roughly a point per year beforehand and 0.3–0.4 points after."
- The piece restricts its own analysis to "whites and blacks" combined and
  does not give separate convergence rates by race.

## Summary

An independent check on the same claim as the TIME/Blair source: gender wage
convergence slowed sharply, from roughly a full percentage point a year before
family-leave mandates to about a third of a point a year after. The exact
figures differ slightly from TIME's summary of Blair's paper (76% slowdown),
which is expected since they're different analyses — both agree convergence
slowed by roughly two-thirds to three-quarters after leave mandates arrived.
