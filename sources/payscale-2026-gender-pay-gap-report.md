# 2026 Gender Pay Gap Report (Payscale)

**URL:** https://www.payscale.com/featured-content/gender-pay-gap
**Date read:** 2026-08-01

## Key figures

Data collected January 2024–January 2026.

- Overall **uncontrolled** gap: women earn **$0.82** per dollar men earn (women earn 18% less overall).
- Overall **controlled** gap (same job title, same qualifications): **$0.99** per dollar.
- By age, uncontrolled / controlled:
  - 20–29: **$0.86** / **$1.00**
  - 30–44: **$0.80** / **$0.98**
  - 45+: **$0.71** / **$0.97**
- Executive level: **$0.69** uncontrolled / **$0.92** controlled.
- By race/ethnicity, uncontrolled / controlled (relative to white men):
  - American Indian/Alaska Native women: **$0.74** / **$0.97**
  - Asian women: **$0.96** / **$1.01**
  - Black/African American women: **$0.78** / **$0.99**
  - Hispanic women: **$0.79** / **$1.00**
  - White women: **$0.82** / **$0.99**

## Quote

> "Women collectively earn 18% less than men based on how they're paid for
> the jobs they have now."

## Summary

Payscale's 2026 report shows the uncontrolled pay gap (what women actually
take home vs. men, ignoring job differences) is much wider than the
controlled gap (same job, same qualifications), meaning most of the raw gap
comes from which jobs and levels women hold, not unequal pay for identical
work — and that gap widens sharply with age and seniority.
